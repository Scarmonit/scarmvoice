Mute a channel for three hours, and a settings screen worth reading

**The bell in the channel header does what a bell should**

- Clicking it used to open the channel's whole menu — rename, delete and all. It opens a proper notification popout now.
- **Mute Channel** sits at the top. Hover it and pick how long: 15 minutes, 1 hour, 3 hours, 8 hours, 24 hours, or until you turn it back on. The channel goes quiet for exactly that long and then comes back on its own, and tells you when it does.
- Under it, the three levels as radio buttons: **All Messages**, **Only @mentions**, **Nothing**.
- Muting and the level are separate on purpose. Set a channel to mentions-only, mute it for an hour, and when the hour is up it is back to mentions-only — not reset to everything.
- The popout says what is currently true: a muted channel shows how much longer, and offers **Unmute Channel** right there. Muted channels also say so in Settings → Notifications, with an Unmute beside each one.
- Renaming and deleting a channel are still on the channel itself — right-click it in the list, or use the bell on its row.

**A Threads button, and a panel that lists them all**

- New button in the channel header, to the left of the bell. It opens a **Threads** panel with a search box and a Create button.
- It lists every thread in the channel — not just the ones in the part of the conversation you happen to have loaded. Each row shows the thread's name, who started it, how many replies, and when the last one was. Click one to open it.
- Type in the box to find a thread by name.
- **Create** asks what the opening message should say and posts it, then opens its thread. A thread here hangs off a message, so that is what starting one means — no empty threads.
- When a channel has none, it says so, and offers the button to start one.

**A whole new Accessibility section**

- Live **Preview** at the top: real messages, drawn the real way. Drag a slider and watch them change.
- **Text size in chat** is a slider from 12px to 24px now, instead of four fixed sizes. Ctrl + and Ctrl − move it a pixel at a time; Ctrl 0 puts it back.
- **Always underline links**, for links that should stand out more.
- **UI Density** — Compact, Default or Spacious — changes the spacing in the channel and member lists.
- **Chat Message Display** — Default or Compact — is the message layout.
- **Space Between Message Groups**, 0 to 24 pixels.
- **Zoom level**, 50% to 200%, for the whole interface.
- **Saturation**, 0% to 100%, for anyone sensitive to strong colour. It deliberately leaves images, video and attachments alone.
- **High Contrast Mode** — brighter text, real borders instead of hairlines, and focus outlines you cannot miss.
- **Reduced Motion**, so you can stop the animations without changing a Windows setting.
- **Announce new messages**, which hands incoming messages to your screen reader.

**Behaviour is now System, and it has your keybinds in it**

- The same startup, tray and auto-join settings, laid out as switches with a line explaining each one.
- New: **Enable Hardware Acceleration**. Turning it off can fix visual glitches. It needs a restart and it asks — it will not restart on you, and it will not pretend to have taken effect either.
- **Custom Keybinds** — push-to-talk, mute and deafen — reachable here as well as from Voice & Audio. They are the same three hotkeys either way, so the two screens can never disagree.
- **Default Keybinds** lists every shortcut built into the app. Only the real ones.

**Notifications, rebuilt**

- Four sections: **Overview**, **Sounds**, **Badges** and **Advanced**.
- Overview: desktop notifications, taskbar flashing, and Do Not Disturb, each with a switch and a sentence.
- Sounds: **New Message**, **New Message in the channel I'm currently reading** (off by default — you are looking at it), **Voice Join & Leave**, and **Disable All Notification Sounds** over the top of them. Every one has a **Preview Sound** link, and the master switch leaves your individual choices alone so turning it off puts them back.
- Badges: the unread count on the taskbar button, separately from the flashing — you can have the number without the pulsing.
- Advanced: every channel's notification level in one list, including any that are muted for a while.

**The Account tab looks the part**

- Restyled to match the rest: an **Account Info** card with your display name, custom status and role, then **Password & Security**.
- Nothing was removed. The profile picture, the display name, the custom status, the board account, two-factor, sign out, disable and delete are all where they were — and **Members** is still there, with Make admin, Ban, Reset password, Clear 2FA and Delete, still owner-only.
- Every settings tab now lists its own headings in the left rail, so you can jump straight to a section instead of scrolling.
