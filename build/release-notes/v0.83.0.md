Voice calls that heal themselves, and an end to the robotic voice

**Dropped voice connections now reconnect on their own**

Sometimes a person in a call would go grey with a warning triangle — still
listed in the channel, but disconnected from the actual audio — and stay that
way until they noticed on their own and rejoined.

The cause: when the connection to the voice server is lost badly enough, the
voice engine inside the app gives up and announces it — and nothing in the
app was listening. The app went on believing it was in the call, kept telling
everyone else so, and the person sat there hearing nothing.

The app now catches that moment and fixes it by itself: it leaves the dead
call cleanly and rejoins automatically, trying at one second, four seconds
and ten seconds so a router hiccup and a Wi-Fi handover are both covered.
Your mute and deafen state come back with you. You will see "connection lost
— reconnecting…" and then "reconnected"; if all three tries fail, the network
is genuinely down and the app says so once instead of pretending.

**The randomly robotic voice is fixed**

Voices would sometimes turn choppy and robotic mid-call, seemingly at random.

The cause: the voice transport silently enables an audio-saving feature (DTX)
that stops sending sound whenever it decides you are silent. Behind the
app's noise suppression that decision is wrong a lot — quiet word endings and
soft talkers fall below its threshold mid-sentence, so the stream cuts in and
out around the edges of speech. That cutting is what sounded robotic, and it
came and went with how quietly someone happened to be talking.

The app now strips that feature from every voice connection. It saved a few
kbps during silence; clear speech is worth more.

Both fixes are live on the website's message board too, so browser and
desktop callers get the same stability.

**In short**

- A dropped voice connection now reconnects automatically within seconds
- Your mute and deafen state survive the reconnect
- The random robotic-voice artifacts are gone (opus DTX disabled)
- Same fixes deployed to voice on the website
