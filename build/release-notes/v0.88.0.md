Forty announcement voices, and a newer speech model behind them

**Pick your announcer from the full catalog**

The single Male/Female toggle for the join/leave announcements is now two
linked dropdowns in Settings → Voice &amp; Audio: a Voice Type (Male or
Female) and a Voice list that shows every voice of that type — sixteen male
and twenty-four female — each with a short character note, like "Orion –
deep, approachable" or "Luna – friendly, conversational". The Preview
button speaks your own greeting in whichever voice is selected, before you
commit to anything.

Your choice is saved with your settings and used for the announcements you
hear in calls — the preview and the real thing are always the same voice.

**A newer speech model underneath**

The announcements now render on the newer generation of the speech model
(Aura-2), which sounds noticeably more natural than the original. Every
voice in the catalog was verified as actually available on the server
before being offered — the list is the model's own, not a wish list.

Costs don't change: one announcement is rendered at a time, each sentence
is still cached at the edge after its first render, and offering more
voices to choose from renders nothing extra.

**In short**

- Two linked dropdowns: Voice Type, then all voices of that type
- 40 voices, each with a short character description
- Preview speaks your greeting in the currently selected voice
- Upgraded to the newer Aura-2 speech model
- Same cost profile — one render per new sentence, cached after
