One smooth gradient across the whole app, steered by a direction slider

**The custom theme's colours blend the way they should now**

Multiple colours used to tint each pane separately, which could never blend
across a pane boundary — the result was uneven patches rather than a fade.
The custom theme now works the way the reference does: your colours become
ONE smooth gradient laid under the entire window, and the surfaces above it
— the sidebars, the chat column, the top bars, the user dock, the composer —
become tinted glass over it. The same sweep reads continuously through the
whole interface.

Color Intensity now controls how transparent that glass is: at zero you have
the stock theme, at full the gradient shines through everything. Menus,
popovers and modals stay solid so they remain readable over whatever is
behind them.

**New: Gradient Direction**

A Gradient Direction slider under Controls (0°–360°) turns the whole
gradient: 0° runs your first colour from the top, 90° from the left, and
everything in between. Surprise Me! now rolls a direction too.

**The colour swatches moved up top**

Your added colours now sit as rounded squares on a strip painted with the
gradient itself, above the colour picker — the selected one highlighted with
a white edge. The strip previews exactly what the window gets.

**Hover names on the theme tiles**

The theme swatches in Settings → Appearance now name themselves on hover —
Light, Ash, Dark, Onyx, Sync with Computer, Custom — in the app's own
tooltip style.

**In short**

- Multiple colours blend as one smooth gradient under the whole window
- New Gradient Direction slider, 0 to 360 degrees
- Colour swatches sit on a gradient strip above the picker
- Theme tiles name themselves on hover
