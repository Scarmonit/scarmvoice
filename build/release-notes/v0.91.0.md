Tinted glass — the theme learns to layer like the reference

**One sheet, many panes**

Custom themes are rebuilt on the reference's real pipeline. Your colours
become one dimmed sheet laid under the whole window, and every panel is a
fixed smoked-glass layer over it: the server rail and the title bar show
the sheet bare, the channel list smokes it lightly, the chat and member
areas sit on a darker slab, and the channel header is the darkest plate of
all. The bottom strip — your name bar and the message box — is nearly
clear on purpose, so the bottom of your gradient glows through it.

Because the glass never changes with your colours, every palette keeps the
same panel-to-panel depth, and white text on the chat area stays readable
whatever you pick.

**Dark picks can't crush to black**

If you choose very dark colours, the sheet is lifted just enough — keeping
your hue — so the darker panes never collapse into unreadable black. On a
light base the whole system flips: the glass becomes paper-white at the
same strengths.

**A five-colour default worth keeping**

The customizer now holds up to five colours, and the default theme is a
five-stop sweep — ember through violet and deep blue into green — tuned so
the rail shows it exactly as measured from the reference.

**In short**

- One dimmed gradient sheet under the window; fixed per-panel glass over it
- The rail, title bar and bottom strip glow; chat sits on the dark slab
- Panel depth and text readability are identical across every palette
- Very dark picks are floored instead of crushing to black
- Light base flips the glass to white at the same strengths
- Up to five colours; a new five-stop default gradient
