The notification sound is the app's own again, emoticons become emoji, and a profile picture opens full size

**Notifications**

- **The new-message sound is ScarmVoice's chime again, not the Windows one.** With the window minimised, a message arriving played the standard Windows notification sound instead of the chime this app has of its own. Two things were behind it. The desktop notification was raised with sound, and a Windows notification with sound plays whatever the operating system has been told to play — over the top of, and louder than, anything the app makes. And a message landing in a channel you were not reading raised that notification and made no sound of its own at all, so the system ding was the whole of what you heard. Notifications are now silent, the app makes the sound, and it makes it for every message: the channel you are in, the channels you are not, and conversations.
- The chime no longer misses the first message of a session. For a moment after startup — the window opening straight into the tray, the connection coming up — the sound file had not finished loading and the chime was simply skipped. There is a fallback behind it now, so a message that arrives in those first seconds is heard like any other.
- Several messages arriving at once are one chime rather than a pile of them playing over each other.
- Turning the chime off, or turning all sounds off, now really does mean silence: it used to leave the Windows sound on the notification behind it.

**Messages**

- **Text emoticons become emoji.** Type `:)` and get 🙂 — and the same for `:(`, `:D`, `;)`, `:P` and the rest of the common ones. The swap happens on the space after the emoticon, and again over the whole message when you send it, so what goes out is the emoji rather than the characters. It works in the message box, in thread replies and in conversations.
- It can be turned off. **Settings › Chat › Formatting › Turn emoticons into emoji** — on by default, remembered, and it takes effect on the very next thing you type.
- **It never touches code.** A smiley inside `backticks` or a fenced code block is nearly always code — `:P` in a regular expression, `:)` in a string — so those are left exactly as they were, whatever the setting says. A code block you are still writing, with no closing fence yet, is left alone too.
- It stays out of the way of everything that only looks like an emoticon: a link (`http://…`), a time (`12:00`) and a custom `:name:` emoji are all untouched, because an emoticon only converts when it stands as a word of its own.

**Profiles**

- **A profile picture opens full size.** Click the big face on the profile panel beside a conversation, or the face at the top of the popout you get from the member list, and the picture opens centred and enlarged. Click outside it, press Escape or use the close button to put it away — and from there you can save or copy it like any other image.
- Nothing else about clicking a face changed: in the member list and on a message, it still opens the profile the way it always has.
