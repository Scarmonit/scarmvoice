Three quiet ones in search and the side panels

**Searching a conversation for itself came back empty**

The search box in a conversation offers **In**, both in its dropdown and under
*More filters*, and it lists the conversations you are in — including the one
you are reading. Choosing it emptied the list:

- Every message vanished and the column said "No loaded messages match these
  filters", for a conversation whose messages all matched.
- The archive results underneath went on showing hits from that same
  conversation, which made it look like the messages had gone rather than the
  filter being wrong.

Choosing this conversation now leaves it as it is, and choosing a different one
narrows to nothing — which is exactly what **In** means when you use it in a
channel.

**The number beside the search box could change on its own**

While you are searching a conversation, the box says how many messages match.
A message arriving in the channel behind the conversation replaced that number
with the channel's — usually "0 matches", over a list that was plainly showing
several.

The count now belongs to whatever you are actually searching.

**The panel edges moved twice per keypress after signing out and back in**

Both side panels can be resized with the arrow keys once the drag handle has
focus. If you signed out and signed back in without closing the app, one press
moved the panel two steps instead of one — three after the next sign-in, and so
on. Dragging with the mouse was unaffected.

One press is one step again, however many times you have signed in.

**Also**

- A stray character in the app's own source made the largest file in the
  project read as binary to search tools, so searching it stopped partway
  through. Nothing about the running app changes; the next person to go looking
  for something in there will find it.
