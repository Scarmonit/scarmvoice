The Threads panel, measured against the real thing

**The header sat on the wrong shade — in two places**

- The Threads panel filled its title bar with a lighter shade than the panel, so the bar read as raised out of it. It is one surface now, separated by a single hairline. The Pinned Messages panel had the identical problem and was fixed last release; both are consistent now.
- The panel is bigger — 602 × 450 rather than 528 × 368 — because the empty state is the whole point of it and it needed the room.

**The search box was a hole, not a field**

- It was a dark well cut into the header with no border at all, twenty-one points darker than the bar it sat in. It is a bordered input now, only slightly darker than the panel, matching every other field in the app after last release's pass on the Filters form.
- The Threads glyph beside the title was a thin scratch. It is heavier and properly sized to the words next to it.

**The empty state was a placeholder; now it is a picture**

- It used to be the header's icon, scaled up slightly. There is a real illustration there now — a disc holding the mark, a gold sparkle to one side and a small blue cluster to the other — at roughly twice the footprint. That composition is most of the difference between "designed" and "missing content".
- "There are no threads." was about 30% too small. It is the right size now.
- The line under it was too dim to read as a sentence and wrapped to three lines in a box far too narrow for it. It is nearly as bright as ordinary message text, in a much wider box, and it fits on one line.
- The whole block is centred in the panel now instead of hugging the header with all the empty space below it, and the Create Thread button is a touch bigger.

**The panel now hangs off the button that opens it**

- It was floating well to the left of the Threads button, with nothing connecting the two. Its right edge now sits just past the button's, so the panel and the button read as one gesture.
- And the button itself lights up white while its panel is open. The members toggle already did this; the Threads button and the bell both did not, so a panel could be open with nothing in the header saying which button had opened it.
