Announcements in the right voice, with no dead air — and restarts that behave

**Everyone hears YOUR voice for YOUR announcement**

The announcement voice was the listener's, not the joiner's: whatever voice
you picked was the voice you heard everyone announced in. Backwards. Your
chosen voice now travels with you — when you join or leave, everyone in the
call hears the announcement in the voice you selected, and you hear theirs
in theirs. The Voice Type and Voice dropdowns now genuinely choose how the
channel hears you.

**The second of dead air is gone**

An announcement was rendered at the moment it was needed, and the network
sat between the event and the sound. The audio is built ahead now:

- Your own join and leave lines render while your pointer is still hovering
  the voice channel, and again whenever you change your words or voice
- Everyone's leave line renders the moment they appear in the call, so a
  departure plays instantly
- A joiner's line starts rendering the moment they are noticed, overlapping
  the short wait for their custom text instead of stacking on top of it —
  and that wait itself got shorter

**The update restart came back to the tray — found and fixed**

The restart fix was being erased by update CHAINS. When several releases
were pending, the middle hop — a process that installs the next update
before ever building a window — answered "not visible" and overwrote the
note your click had written. Single updates worked; catching up over two or
more broke. Only a process that actually owns a window may answer now, so
the note survives the whole chain and the app comes back open.

**In short**

- Join/leave announcements play in the voice of the person they are about
- Announcement audio is pre-rendered, so it plays promptly
- Restart-to-install restores your window even across several queued updates
