The notice about your own permissions now waits to be read

**Being told your role changed, and losing it to the next click**

When someone gives you a new role — or takes one away — the app tells you in a
box with a single OK button, because it is a change to what you are allowed to
do and it can arrive while you are reading something else entirely.

It could be dismissed by clicking anywhere. That is the problem: the notice
appears over whatever you were already doing, and the very next click was aimed
at something underneath it — a message, a channel, the composer. That click
closed the notice instead, often before there was time to read a word of it, and
nothing in the app will show it to you again. Pressing Escape did the same.

The box is now static. Clicking beside it does nothing except put the highlight
back on the button, and Escape does nothing at all. The only way past it is OK,
which is what a notice you are meant to have read should require.

**What that means in practice**

- **A click beside the box** moves the highlight onto OK rather than closing
  anything, so the click still does something visible and points at the way out.
- **Escape is refused, not passed along.** It no longer slips by the notice to
  close a panel or a picker sitting behind it, leaving the notice up over
  something that has just vanished.
- **Every other prompt is untouched.** Renaming, deleting, passwords and the
  rest still close on Escape and on a click outside, exactly as before. Only a
  notice with nothing to decline holds you there.
- **Signing out still clears it,** as it clears everything else — a notice you
  never acknowledged does not follow you into the next session.
