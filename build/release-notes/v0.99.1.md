Two rough edges on the new Watch Stream controls

**Fixes**

- **Stop Watching no longer shows two tooltips at once.** Hovering it produced the app's own tooltip and then, a moment later, the operating system's on top of it. It now uses the same single tooltip as the Pop out and Fullscreen buttons beside it.
- **A presenter's face on their LIVE tile now keeps up with their profile picture.** If somebody changed their picture while they were streaming, their tile kept drawing the old one until the stream ended and started again.

Everything from v0.99.0 — a screen share you have to opt into, and picking between somebody's screen and their camera — is unchanged.
