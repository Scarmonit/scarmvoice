The search filters stay on top, and the Accessibility preview shows what it should

**Fixed: the panel drag line cut through the search filters**

- Open the search box in the top right and the filters list appears over the member list. The line you drag to resize that panel was drawing straight through the list — and worse, it was taking your pointer with it: hovering the filters where the line crossed them grabbed the resizer instead, and a drag there resized the panel while you were trying to pick a filter.
- The dropdown now sits above the drag line completely. Hovering or clicking anywhere inside it reaches the dropdown and nothing else, and the line no longer appears across it. Dragging the panel still works everywhere the dropdown is not.

**"Space Between Message Groups" lands on real values**

- It had the problem "Text size in chat" used to have: going from 8px to 16px came to rest on 12px on the way, a spacing no tick names. It now snaps straight onto each marked value — 0, 4, 8, 16, 24 — and never rests between them.
- Every stepped slider in Settings is held to the same rule.
- The arrow keys now walk from one tick to the next on those sliders. They used to be a dead end at some values: pressing right on "Text size in chat" at 12px moved to 13, which snapped back to 12, so the key did nothing at all. Home and End jump to the ends.

**Slider tracks are the right thickness, and the tick marks run through the bar**

- Every track in Settings was drawn at twice the thickness it should be. All of them are corrected, including Microphone Volume and Speaker Volume in Voice & Audio.
- The tick marks were short marks sitting near the bar. They are now full-height lines running through it — above the bar, across it and below it — at each labelled value.
- A side effect worth having: the slider handle can be grabbed from anywhere on it now. The scale of numbers above the track was quietly intercepting clicks aimed at the top half of the handle.

**The Preview at the top of Accessibility**

- It is **two messages** now, each with its own name and time, instead of everything bundled under one header. That is also what gives the spacing slider something to move: with one group there was no gap between groups to see.
- The username is shown in a colour, as a name with a role would be.
- Beside the first message there is a stack of three status faces — online, idle, do not disturb — each with its own badge shape as well as its own colour, which is the point of them. Beside the second there is an example button, the app's own, at whatever text size you have chosen.
- It stays live while you work: text size, spacing, message display, UI density, zoom, saturation, high contrast and reduced motion all show up in it as you move the control, so you can see what a setting does before you leave the page.

**Unselected options are quieter**

- The ring on an unselected radio button was a light grey that competed with the filled blue one next to it, so a set of choices had no obvious answer. It is dark now, and the selected one is slightly larger with a bigger dot. Hovering an unselected option still brightens it.
