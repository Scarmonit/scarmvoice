"View Full Profile" is a profile now

**It was showing you less than the panel it opened from**

Clicking **View Full Profile** gave you a small grey box with the person's name as a title, one line reading "Username: … Account id: … Role: …", and two buttons — Cancel and Close — that both did the same thing. The side panel beside it was already showing more than that.

It was the confirm dialog being reused: a shell shaped title → body → Cancel/Confirm. That shape is for a decision, and a profile is a thing to look at.

**What it is now**

A proper card, about three times taller and eight times the area:

- The person's **banner**, with their **picture over its lower edge** and their status on it — the same arrangement the side panel uses, one size up.
- Their **name as the subject of the view**, large, with the handle under it. It used to be the smallest thing on the card.
- A **Message** button that opens the conversation with them — where "Close" used to sit. Plus **Block** (or **Unblock**) when there is something to act on.
- The facts stacked as **label over value** — Status, Role on this board, Account — instead of three labelled values run together on one line. Your side panel already did it this way; the modal now agrees with it.
- A **✕** in the corner. No Cancel, no second dismiss button: Escape and clicking outside work as well.

**One colour for filled buttons**

The app was using two: the Threads popover's Create, the settings radios and every slider are blue-violet, while dialog confirm buttons were teal. Filled primary buttons are all the same blue-violet now. The teal accent keeps everything it was already doing — links, active state, and the brand itself.
