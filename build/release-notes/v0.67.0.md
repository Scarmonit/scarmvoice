Fixed: the header didn't switch when you opened a conversation

**The channel header stayed behind in your DMs**

This is the one that mattered. Open a direct message and the header at the top still showed the last text channel you had been reading — "# random", with the channel buttons beside it — while the sidebar, the conversation and the profile panel all correctly showed the person you were talking to.

It was a regression from v0.64.0. The channel header was moved in front of the member list so the search filters would stop being cut through by the panel's drag handle; that also moved it in front of the conversation panel, which is supposed to replace the whole right-hand side — header included. The conversation panel now sits in front of it again, so the header shows the person you are talking to, with their picture beside their name.

**The conversation list**

- **"Find or start a conversation"** is drawn as a button now — filled, taller, with its label centred and readable. It used to look like a search field you type into, which is not what it does: it opens a picker.
- **"Direct Messages"** matches the "Text Channels" heading in the server sidebar. It was still the old small all-caps style, so the two sidebars — which occupy the same column and swap for each other — disagreed with each other.
- Fixed: the **Members** row at the top of the list was drawing the browser's own button plate, a light rectangle in the middle of a dark sidebar.

**The person you're talking to**

- The **handle** under someone's name — in the profile panel and in the block at the top of a conversation — was drawn dim, which turned the second half of somebody's identity into a caption. It is at full brightness now, and the **@** in front of it is gone.
- The **Account** line in the profile panel is brighter for the same reason: it is a fact about the person, not a hint about the label above it.
- The profile panel's edge was a light seam against a dark panel; it is a proper hairline now, and the **View Full Profile** button is the reference's height.
- The block at the top of a conversation now carries an **actions row** under the sentence, as the reference does — **View Profile**, and **Block** or **Unblock**. Block only appears when this app can act on it: blocking here is a local mute of a specific install rather than a server-side relationship, so a person nobody has seen online yet has nothing to block, exactly as the profile popout already behaves. A group shows **Leave Group** instead.
