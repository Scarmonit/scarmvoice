Voice announcements that say who arrived — in your own words

**Joins and leaves are spoken now**

The static "Greetings" and "See ya around" clips are gone. When someone
joins the voice channel, everyone in it hears a spoken announcement —
"scarmonit has joined the channel" — and when someone leaves, "scarmonit
has left the channel". The person leaving hears their own send-off too.

The speech is generated on your machine as it happens, from the actual
username of whoever arrived. Nothing is recorded and nothing leaves your
computer.

**Greeting & Leaving — your own words**

Settings → Voice & Audio has a new Greeting & Leaving section with two
boxes. Leave them empty and the channel hears your username. Fill them in
and your words replace your name: set your Greeting to "The Boss" and the
channel hears "The Boss has joined the channel". Letters and spaces only,
up to 20 characters — and what you set travels with you, so everyone else's
app speaks it, not just yours.

**Pick the voice**

Choose a male or female voice for the announcements you hear, with a
Preview button beside it. The choice is yours locally — each person picks
the voice their own announcements are read in.

**In short**

- Joins and leaves are announced by name, spoken aloud, for everyone in
  the call
- Your custom Greeting and Leaving texts stand in for your name everywhere
- Male or female announcement voice, with preview
- The person leaving hears their own announcement
- The old Voice Join & Leave toggle now governs these announcements, and
  its Preview button speaks yours
