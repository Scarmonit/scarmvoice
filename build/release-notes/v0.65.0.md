A Light theme that was designed, not inverted

**The Light theme has been rebuilt from scratch**

The old one was the dark theme turned upside down — the settings page even said so, "the same layout, inverted". That is why it looked harsh: inverting a dark theme gives you a flat white app with near-black text on it, and none of the depth the dark theme gets from its layered shades.

It has its own palette now.

**Layered off-whites instead of a sheet of white**

- Nothing that makes up the page is pure white any more. The server rail is the deepest tone, the channel and member lists sit above it, and the message column above that — so the areas of the app are told apart by their shade, the way they are in the dark theme.
- The member list is its own subtle step away from the conversation beside it. Before, the two were the same white with a hairline between them, which read as one big sheet.
- The only things that are white are the things that float: menus, modals, popovers, tooltips, the message box and the settings window. That is what makes a menu look like it is sitting in front of the app rather than being another patch of it.
- Soft lines separate panels and outline the floating surfaces, and the shadows under them are gentle and warm rather than hard black.

**Text you can read without squinting**

- No more near-black. Message text is a soft dark grey, names and headings a shade darker than that, and timestamps and category labels a lighter grey — a real hierarchy instead of everything at maximum contrast.
- Every piece of text on the page was measured against the surface it sits on. The quiet ones are still comfortably legible; channel names in particular used to be right at the edge.

**Softer colour**

- The coloured circles behind people's initials were tuned for a dark background and shouted on a light one. They are gentler now, and so is the band across the top of a profile card, which used to be almost black.
- Status dots, reaction pills and the accent colour were all darkened by the old inversion until they punched holes in the page. They sit at a normal weight now, and the accent is dark enough to read as a link on white.
- Switches, radio buttons, sliders and their tick marks all got light-theme values of their own. An off switch used to be a near-white well with a white knob in it — a switch with nothing in it.
- Offline members faded almost to nothing against a light background. They now read as dimmed rather than absent.

**Things that were still dark in the Light theme**

Several parts of the app had their colours written into them individually, so no theme could reach them: the pinned-message cards, the fields and buttons in the search Filters window, the selected page in the Settings sidebar, the slider tick marks, and the outlines of the Threads and Pinned panels. All of them follow the theme now.

Also fixed: keyboard focus used to draw a hard black box around buttons in the Light theme — that was the browser's default ring showing through. It is the app's own accent colour now, in both themes.

**The Dark theme is unchanged.** Every value in it was preserved exactly; this release only gives the light one values of its own.
