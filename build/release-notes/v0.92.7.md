A message that could stop the channel, and an update that stopped offering itself

**Messages**

- **One message can no longer freeze the channel for everybody.** A message made of nothing but quote marks, thousands of them in a row, was drawn by opening a quote inside a quote for every single one, until the app ran out of room and gave up mid-draw. The message never appeared, nor did any message below it, and every refresh after that failed at the same place: the conversation simply stopped updating for everyone who had that message on screen, until it aged off the page. Quoting now stops nesting after twelve levels and shows the rest as plain text, which no real message ever reaches.
- A message that fails to send no longer eats what you have typed since. The text you sent is handed back when the server refuses it — but an upload can take minutes, and anything you had typed in the meantime was silently replaced by it. Both are kept now.

**Updates**

- **"Check for updates" while offline no longer hides an update you already have.** If a new version had finished downloading and was waiting for you to restart, a check that could not reach the internet reported the failure over the top of it: the update banner disappeared entirely, and a "Restart now" you had already pressed was quietly forgotten when your call ended. The failed check is now only a failed check.
- An update waiting to be installed stops re-downloading itself. Every five minutes it went back to "0% downloaded", sat there re-checking bytes it already had, and clicking it during that window put the restart off until it finished.
- **Double-clicking the shortcut on update day does something again.** If you run ScarmVoice minimised to the tray, launching it while an update was downloading showed nothing at all — no window, no tray icon, no update screen — for as long as the download took. It now shows the update screen, and comes back on screen afterwards instead of going quietly into the tray.

**Direct messages**

- Editing a message in a conversation and then closing the conversation no longer freezes your channels. The half-open editor stayed behind where nothing could see it, and the channel list stopped showing new messages, edits and reactions for the rest of the session.
- A conversation you have read all the way back stops growing its "Load earlier messages" button back every twelve seconds — over a conversation with nothing earlier left, above a button that did nothing when pressed.

**Voice**

- Being disconnected while ScarmVoice was reconnecting no longer says "reconnected". Signing out, or being taken over by another device, in the second or two the app spends getting back into a call left it announcing a reconnection that never happened, with the mute and deafen buttons lit for a call that was over.

**Edit Mode**

- Opening Edit Layout while you are in Direct Messages no longer throws the conversation list into a small box in the middle of the window — where it could not be selected, moved or reset.
- Renaming a layout only renames it. It used to save every move you had made since opening the editor along with the name, and answering "don't save" on the way out could not take those back.

**Under the hood**

- A picture link in a message can no longer be used to reach services running on your own machine or network. One spelling of a local address slipped past the check that exists to stop exactly that.
- Your own message reliably appears in the channel right after you send it, even when the refresh behind it fails — the app remembers that it still owes itself a fresh read instead of accepting a stale one.
