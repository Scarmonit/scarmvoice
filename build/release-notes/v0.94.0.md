Slash commands, Clear Channel, and a member popout that offers everyone the same things

**Slash commands**

- **Type `/` in an empty message box and a command menu opens**, the way it does in every other chat app. Keep typing to filter it, and Enter, Tab or a click runs the one that is highlighted. It uses the same popup as the @mention list, so it looks and behaves exactly like the one you already know.
- The commands: **/profile** opens your profile, **/settings** opens settings, **/dm [user]** opens a conversation with someone (or the picker if you don't name anyone), **/mute** and **/deafen** toggle your microphone and audio, **/leave** leaves the call, **/theme** opens the theme customiser, and **/shrug** drops ¯\\\_(ツ)\_/¯ into your message.
- **A slash you meant literally is still a slash.** The menu only appears when `/` is the very first character of an otherwise-empty box, on a single line, followed by one plain word. So `https://example.com`, `/home/user/notes`, `C:\Users\you`, `and/or`, `10/08/2026` and anything inside a code block are all left completely alone — and `/somethingmadeup` is sent as the ordinary message it is, rather than being refused.

**Channels**

- **Clear Channel.** The channel's own menu (right-click it, or the bell beside it) now has **Clear all messages**, which deletes every message in that channel — replies, reactions and attachments with them — while keeping the channel itself. It is offered on **#general**, which is the one that cannot be deleted and the one that fills up.
- It is the **owner's** alone, and the server enforces that rather than the app merely hiding the button. It asks first, and says plainly that the messages go for **everyone** and that it cannot be undone. The toast afterwards tells you how many were deleted.

**Profiles**

- **"View Full Profile" is on the member popout.** Clicking somebody in the member list gave you Message, Mention and Block, and no way at all to reach their full profile card from there.
- **The popout offers everybody the same things.** It used to quietly show a different set of actions for different people — Block was there for anyone online and missing for anyone offline, with nothing on screen explaining why. It now works out who somebody is from everything the app knows, including the messages already on screen, so an offline member's popout is the same menu as anybody else's. Ban and Remove-from-voice still depend on your role and on there being a call, which is the one thing that should vary.
- **The big face on the full profile card opens the picture full size too**, matching the profile panel and the member popout. Clicking a face anywhere else still opens the profile, exactly as before.
