Switching to Compact now reaches what is already on screen

**The setting took a moment to reach an open conversation or thread**

The previous release taught direct messages and threads to follow *Settings →
Accessibility → Chat Message Display*. It taught them a little too late: turning
Compact on while a conversation or a thread was actually open changed the
spacing straight away but left the messages themselves drawn the old way —
bundled under one header, with the avatar column now gone. Lines written by the
same person in the same minute lost their name and their timestamp until
somebody said something new in that conversation.

The channel column never had this, because it redraws itself whenever the
setting changes. The other two only redraw when a message arrives, and a quiet
conversation can stay quiet for a long time.

Both are redrawn the moment the setting moves now, so what is on screen matches
what was just chosen.
