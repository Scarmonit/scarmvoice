Update Available, without going looking for it

**A new version now announces itself**

If an update is published while you have ScarmVoice open, an **Update Available** pill appears at the top of the window on its own — usually within a second or two of the release going live. You no longer have to go into Settings and press *Check for Updates* to find out.

It works two ways, so one of them always does:

- The release **tells** the app. Publishing a version sends a message down the same live connection the board already uses, and every open copy of ScarmVoice checks straight away.
- Failing that, the app **asks** — every five minutes, and again whenever it comes back from the tray, wakes from sleep or reconnects. That used to be every three hours, which is why an update usually turned up at the next launch instead.

**One click, and you are on the new version**

The pill is a filled blue-violet bar across the top — hard to miss, and the whole bar is clickable, not just the button on the end.

- Click it and the update installs and the app reopens on the new version. That is the whole flow.
- You can click it while it is still downloading. It remembers, and applies the update the moment the download finishes — you never have to press anything twice.
- It says the same thing — *Update Available* — from the moment one is found until it is installed, rather than renaming itself three times on the way.

**It no longer restarts you without asking**

Previously an update that arrived mid-session downloaded and then restarted the app by itself, whatever you happened to be doing. Now it waits for you.

- Nothing restarts until you click.
- Ignoring the pill is safe: the update still applies the next time you close ScarmVoice, so you cannot fall behind by leaving it alone.
- If you are in a call, the pill says so and waits — and if you press it anyway, it believes you.
- Updates found **at launch** are unchanged: they still install before the app opens, because there is nothing to interrupt yet.
