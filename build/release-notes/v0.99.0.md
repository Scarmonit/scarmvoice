Nobody gets dropped into your stream any more

**Watching a screen share is now your choice**

- **A screen share no longer takes over the window of everyone in the call.** Until now, the moment somebody started sharing, their screen appeared on your stage and their desktop audio started playing, whether you wanted it or not, with no way to say no. Now it is offered instead: you decide whether to look.
- **Somebody going live appears as a tile above the conversation** with their name, a red LIVE badge and a **Watch Stream** button. Nothing plays until you press it. There is a LIVE badge beside their name in the voice list too, and **Watch Stream** in the menu you get by clicking them there.
- **Stop Watching puts the stream away** without touching the person sharing — they keep streaming, and their tile keeps standing by if you want to come back.
- **You hear a presenter's desktop audio only while you are watching them.** Sound arrives and leaves with the picture, which is what it does everywhere else.
- **A stream you have not opted into is not being decoded on your machine at all.** Declining costs you nothing.

**Screen or camera — you pick**

- **Somebody streaming their screen *and* their camera is two separate things to watch, and you choose between them.** Both appear as their own tile and their own pill in the strip above the video, and their popout menu offers **Watch Stream** and **Watch Camera** separately.
- Switching is one click, and clicking whatever is already playing stops it.
- When the stream you were watching ends, the stage now **closes** instead of helping itself to whoever else happens to be presenting.

**Your own share still opens by itself**

- Starting a share opens its preview, because you just asked for it and that is where you check what the rest of the call is being sent. **Hide** closes it, and it stays closed until you ask for it back.
- The quality, motion and Stop controls are unchanged and still only appear over your own share.
