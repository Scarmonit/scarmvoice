Restarts come back where you left them

**Updates and restarting**

- Clicking Restart on an update now brings the app back exactly the way it was when you clicked — visible if you were looking at it, in the tray only if that is where it already was. The old intermittence had a deep cause: the window's "ready to show" signal sometimes never arrives at all, and everything about appearing waited on that one signal. Appearing now takes the first of three independent routes, so it cannot silently never happen.
- Launching the app by hand always shows the window now, even with "start minimized to the tray" turned on. That setting now means exactly what it says — how the app comes up on its own at Windows sign-in — not what happens when you double-click the icon yourself.
- An update that installs at launch (the "Installing update…" screen) now comes back visible when you started the app yourself, and stays in the tray when Windows started it in the background.

**Voice**

- A brief connection hiccup during a call no longer drops you out of it. The connection now heals itself in place — usually within a second or two, sometimes without a single audible gap — and the full automatic rejoin only runs when the connection genuinely cannot recover on its own.
- The exact moment of any voice disconnect is now recorded in the log: who closed the connection, how, and after how long. If the periodic drops continue, the very next one will say on paper whether it comes from this machine or from the server side.
