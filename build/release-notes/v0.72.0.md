Direct messages get everything a channel has

**New Message**

The **+** beside *Direct Messages* opens a proper **New Message** window now — hover it and it says *Create Message*.

- Search the board's members, and tick as many as you like.
- Each row shows their picture, the name they post under and the account underneath it.
- One person opens the conversation you already have with them. Several start a group — up to 10 people, said at the top of the window rather than as an error afterwards.
- **Find or start a conversation** is untouched and still does what it did: it is the one that jumps you to a channel.

**Pinned Messages, Notification Settings and Threads are in a conversation now**

The three buttons at the top of a text channel are at the top of a direct message too — they are the *same* three buttons, so they behave and look exactly the way they do in a channel.

- **Pinned Messages** — pin anything in a conversation and it gets the *pinned* tag in the feed and a card in the panel, with Jump and Unpin. Everyone in a conversation can pin, because everyone in one is an equal.
- **Notification Settings** — All Messages, Only @mentions or Nothing, and Mute Conversation for 15 minutes through to "until I turn it back on". It is set **per conversation**, so silencing a noisy group does not silence the channel behind it.
- **Threads** — start a thread off any message, read and reply to it in the same drawer, and see every thread in the conversation from the Threads panel. The drawer opens beside the conversation instead of vanishing behind it.

**The right-click menu is the same menu**

React, Reply, Reply in thread, Pin, Copy text, Save attachment, Edit, Delete and Block are all there, on the hover bar and on right-click, in a conversation exactly as in a channel. Four of them used to be listed and then answer "Not found" when you picked them.

- **Reactions** work, and they are counted per person rather than per computer — react on your laptop and your desktop and it is one reaction, not two.
- **Reply** quotes the message above your own, including when you send a file with it.
- **Block** works from a conversation now. It was quietly filing an id nothing else in the app matched.

**One thing is deliberately different**

An admin cannot edit or delete somebody else's message inside a private conversation. Moderation is for shared spaces; two people talking privately is not one. Everything else is the same.

**Smaller things**

- Compact mode reaches the conversation column. It stayed roomy while the channel behind it went compact.
- Opening a conversation, or leaving one, now closes any pinned/threads/notification panel that was describing where you just were — it used to sit there still listing the old place.
