Find out why your microphone sounds bad

**Outgoing audio diagnostics**

- **The app now measures the audio you send, continuously, while you are in a call.** Every two seconds it records how many packets actually left, how many the other end says never arrived, the jitter and round trip they measured, how long packets waited before going out, what your upload can carry, how much audio your microphone actually produced, and whether the noise filter kept up.
- **When it sees the send path go bad, it writes a report by itself.** The report covers the run-up as well as the bad moment, so you can see it degrade rather than just see it broken. It waits for two bad readings in a row before writing anything, and writes at most one report a minute.
- **`/voicecheck` saves a report on demand.** Type it in the message box while somebody is telling you that you sound robotic — the person listening to you is a better judge of your microphone than any threshold, and this turns "that sounded bad just then" into a timestamped record. It works for a few minutes after you hang up, too. Reports go to the log: Settings › About › Open Logs.
- **The report names the layer at fault rather than just showing numbers.** Packets that left normally but never arrived are reported as an upload problem; packets that were never produced while your microphone still had signal are reported as a capture or encoder stall on your machine; audio the noise filter could not deliver in time is reported against the noise filter. Those three sound identical to the person listening and have completely different fixes, which is the whole reason for measuring.
- The noise suppressor now keeps its own health counters and reports any moment it missed its deadline, so a hole punched in your audio before it ever reached the network is visible as such.
- The report also reads back the codec that was actually negotiated, so if the fix that disabled Opus DTX in v0.83.0 ever stops taking — that setting alone makes voices sound robotic — it will say so instead of leaving it to be guessed at.
