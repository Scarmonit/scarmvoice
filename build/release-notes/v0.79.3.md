Edit Layout: a popup you can move, and an Orientation that works

**The options popup can be pushed out of the way**

Right-clicking a section in Edit Layout opens its options on top of that
section — which is the one thing you are trying to look at while you change it.
There was no way to move the popup, so you were adjusting something you could
not see.

Drag it by its title bar, the same way the Edit Mode panel has always moved. And
it stays where you put it: right-click a different section and the popup opens
where you left it rather than jumping back over the thing it is about. Its
position is remembered between sessions.

**Orientation actually turns the section now**

Switching a section between Horizontal and Vertical did nothing you could see.
The contents really were restacking — but the space they were restacking into
could not grow, so the new arrangement was drawn outside the section and clipped
away. The channel header had a fixed 48-pixel row and the servers rail a fixed
72-pixel column, so for those two the change was completely invisible; a me bar
turned vertical in a custom layout stacked its name over its controls and
dropped the controls straight through the bottom of its own card.

A section turned onto its other axis is now given the room that axis needs:

- **Vertical stacks the contents** and the section grows tall enough to hold
  them.
- **Horizontal lays them back out in a row** and the section goes back to the
  height it had.
- **It works for every section that offers it** — the me bar, the channel
  header, the message box and the servers rail.
- **In a custom layout the section is re-fitted** to the axis you turned it
  onto, so nothing spills out of its panel any more.
- **It applies the moment you click it**, and it is saved with the layout —
  including in saved layout templates, alongside position, size and show/hide.

**Also**

- Dragging the Edit Mode panel and then touching Show Grid no longer forgets
  where you put the panel.
