Message anyone from where you see them

Suggested by one of you: being able to right-click a name and just start talking to them, instead of going and finding them in a list.

**Direct messages**

- Right-click any message — anywhere on it, including the avatar — and you can message the person who wrote it.
- Clicking someone in the members list, or their tile in a call, offers the same thing at the top of the card that appears.
- Either way it opens the conversation you already have with that person, rather than starting a second one beside it.

It stays out of the way where it could not mean anything: your own messages, messages written before accounts existed, and inside a one-to-one conversation, where the only thread it could open is the one already on screen. Inside a group it is there, because taking one person aside is exactly when you want it.
