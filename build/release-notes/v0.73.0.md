Search works the same everywhere

**Direct messages have the real search bar now**

A conversation used to have its own little search box: it matched the text of whatever messages happened to be loaded, and that was the whole of it. No filters, no dropdown, no *More filters*, and nothing that searched further back than what was on screen.

That box is gone. The search bar from the top of a channel **moves into the conversation header**, so a direct message gets the actual search — the same box, the same menu, the same behaviour:

- **from:** someone, **has:** link / embed / file / image / video / audio, **mentions:** someone, **pinned:**, **before: / after: / during:** a date, and **author:** — all of them, typed or picked from the dropdown.
- **More filters** opens the same form it opens in a channel.
- The box tells you how many messages matched, and clicking one clears the filters and drops you back at it in context.
- **Escape** clears it and **Ctrl+F** reaches it, which in a conversation it never used to.

**…and it searches the whole conversation, not just what is on screen**

Underneath the filters there is now an archive search for direct messages, the way channels have always had one. It looks through everything ever sent in the conversation — not only the messages currently loaded — and the toggle beside the results switches between **This conversation** and **All conversations**.

Results say which conversation each hit came from, and clicking one takes you to that message. A hit inside a thread opens the thread.

**In a conversation, the filters mean conversation things**

- **from:** and **mentions:** offer the people in *that* conversation, rather than everyone who has ever posted in a channel behind it.
- **in:** offers your conversations rather than the channel list, and picking one points the archive search at it.
