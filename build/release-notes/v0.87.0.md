Announcements in a natural voice, and custom greetings everyone actually hears

**Your custom greeting reaches everyone now**

Setting a custom Greeting or Leaving text mostly only worked for you —
everyone else still heard your username. Two bugs, both fixed:

- The texts arrive over the live connection, but the regular presence poll
  overwrote them every few seconds with rows that never carried them. The
  app now remembers what the live connection said, so the poll can no
  longer erase it.
- A joiner shows up in the call a beat before their text arrives, and the
  announcement used to fire at first sight — always with the username.
  Arrivals now wait a moment and speak whatever text has arrived by then.

A join that vanishes within that moment — a connection blip — is not
announced at all, in either direction.

**The voice is a real voice now**

The announcements were using Windows' built-in speech voices, which are the
robotic 1990s sound — the modern natural voices are not available to apps.
Announcements are now spoken by a neural voice (rendered on your server, a
different one for Male and Female), so they sound like a person and
pronounce names properly. If the network is down the old voice steps in,
and if even that is missing you get the classic chime — an arrival never
goes silent.

A bonus from the change: announcements now play through the speaker you
chose in Settings, where the old voice could only ever use the Windows
default output.

**In short**

- Custom Greeting and Leaving texts are heard by the whole channel, reliably
- Natural neural voices for Male and Female instead of the robotic ones
- Announcements follow your chosen output device
- A momentary join-and-drop no longer announces anything
