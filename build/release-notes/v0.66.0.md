Log Out where you expect it, tooltips that stop covering things, and a scale pass

**One Log Out, at the bottom of the settings sidebar**

- There were two "Sign out" buttons in the Account page, a scroll apart, doing different things: one signed you out of your account only, the other out of everything. Both are gone.
- In their place there is a single red **Log Out** with a door icon, pinned to the bottom of the settings sidebar under every other tab. It signs you out of everything, as the lower of the two used to.
- The account-only one is not lost — it is **Switch Accounts**, in the panel over your own name, which is where it reads as what it actually does.

**Tooltips open sideways on the server rail**

- Hovering something in the left rail used to open its label *downwards*, which is exactly where the next item in the rail sits — so the label covered the thing below it. Rail tooltips now open to the right, vertically centred on the icon, with a gap between the pointer and what it is pointing at.
- Everywhere else they still open vertically, which is right for a horizontal row like the channel-header icons.
- The tooltip itself was rebuilt to match: bigger label, a proper outline so it separates from whatever is behind it, symmetric padding (the text used to sit low in the box), and a deeper, more tapered pointer instead of a small nub.

**A crown on the board owner**

The member list now marks the owner with a small gold crown beside their name, the way the reference marks a server owner. Only the owner — admins do not get one, because a list with three crowns in it says nothing.

**Sizes, measured**

Several things were leaning a few percent small, and one was leaning large:

- The channel names in the sidebar, the member names, and the icons along the channel header were all 4–15% under. They are all corrected.
- The "Welcome to #channel!" heading was 17% too large, and the circled **#** above it was too big while the glyph inside it was too small — the disc is smaller now and the mark inside it fills more of it, which is the proportion it should have had.
- The **#** beside the channel name in the header was drawn at full white, so it read as part of the title rather than as the marker that says what kind of channel you are in. It is dimmer now.
