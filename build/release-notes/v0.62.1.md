Pinned messages, measured against the real thing

A pass over the details, from measurements rather than impressions.

**The Pinned Messages panel**

- The header was filled with a lighter shade than the panel — the same shade as the cards below it — so the bar read as raised out of the panel instead of being part of it. It is one surface now, with a single hairline under the title.
- "Pinned Messages" was about 30% too small, and the pin beside it was a 9×13 speck against it. Both are 22px now, so they read as one mark and one word.
- Cards are taller, have their own outline, and sit on their own shade rather than the header's. The panel is a little narrower and no longer collapses to a squat box when there is only one pin.
- The ✕ in the header is gone. Escape, a click anywhere outside, the pin button and switching channel all close it — and it was the only ✕ in the panel, which made the one on a card look like "close" rather than "unpin".
- On a card, **Jump** now comes first, because it is what you opened the panel for. Unpin is a small ✕ beside it instead of the word, which had been the widest thing on the card.

**A pinned message in the channel**

- The tinted background and the accent bar down the side of a pinned message are gone. The small **pinned** tag next to its timestamp is the whole marker now.
- Two reasons. It did not scale: ten pins meant ten permanently highlighted rows scattered through the scrollback, and the channel stopped reading as one conversation. And the treatment was already in use for two other things — a reply quote and the flash that marks a message you have jumped to — so three different states all looked the same.

**The scrollbar**

- It was drawn *darker* than the background it sat on, which reads as a groove with nothing in it. The handle is lighter than its surroundings now, the way a handle should be.
- It also hides itself when nothing is happening, and comes back when you scroll or move the pointer over the list. No more permanent bar down the edge of the conversation.

**"(edited)"**

- It used to drop onto a line of its own underneath the message. It now trails the last words of the text, where it belongs. After a code block or a list — where there is no line to trail — it still goes underneath, because there is nowhere else sensible.
- The two versions, "(edited)" and "(edited by …)", were rendering at different brightnesses. They are the same footnote at the same weight now; the one naming somebody is italic, which is the difference that actually matters.
