The corner comes together — and the customizer stands on its own

**The bottom-left corner is one piece now**

Under a custom theme the voice panel, the buttons and your own name bar
were breaking into separately-shaded blocks, floating brighter than the
column above them. The whole corner now wears the same glass as the
sidebar and runs flush to the window edges — one continuous surface, with
just a hairline between the voice section and your name.

The message box beside it has its own translucent plate again, so there is
a clear gap between your name bar and where you type instead of the two
running together.

**The theme customizer is one solid panel**

With a colourful theme applied, the "Customize your theme" panel was
taking on the theme's own hues — its background and its controls landing
on different colours, so it read as contrasting patches. The panel keeps
one solid neutral surface now, whatever it is previewing — the way the
reference draws it.

It also reaches the very top of the window, with the window buttons
sitting neatly on its head instead of cutting it short, and their strip
matches the panel's colour instead of leaving a wrong-coloured notch.

**Back to Settings goes back to where you were**

The panel's footer button used to drop you on the Account page. It returns
you to Appearance, at the theme section — the place you left from.

**Settings stays solid under any theme**

With a custom theme applied, the settings screen was turning transparent
and the app showed through it. It keeps a fully opaque, theme-matched
backdrop now.

**In short**

- The voice panel, buttons and name bar are one seamless section
- A visible gap between your name bar and the message box
- The customizer panel is one solid colour, full height, clear of the
  window buttons
- Back to Settings lands on Appearance's theme section
- The settings screen is opaque under every theme
