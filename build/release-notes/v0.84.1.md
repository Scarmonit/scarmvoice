The theme customizer now shows you the theme

**The customizer is a side panel now, and the app is the live preview**

The custom theme picker shipped as a centered window over a dimmed backdrop
— which sat on top of the very thing it was theming. Your colours were being
applied the whole time; the dimming just hid them until you closed the
panel, so you were picking blind.

The customizer is now a panel docked to the right edge, the way it should
have been from the start. The app stays fully visible and undimmed beside
it, and every adjustment — a drag on the colour square, a nudge of the
intensity slider — repaints the whole app the moment you make it. Opening
the customizer steps out of Settings so you can see the app; a Back to
Settings button at the bottom returns you.

**And the panel itself got a once-over**

- Surprise Me!, Reset, Add Color and Back to Settings are real filled
  buttons now, not bare labels
- The hex field is one control, with the colour swatch and the eyedropper
  inset inside its border
- Section headings (Appearance, Colors, Controls) are brighter and in Title
  Case; the panel title is smaller and brighter, with a palette glyph
- The stray horizontal scrollbar along the bottom is gone
