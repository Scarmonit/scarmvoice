Custom themes at full strength, and a picker that stops covering the app

**The colours actually come through now**

Measured against the reference, the custom theme's surfaces were passing
about a third of the colour they should at any given intensity — a theme
that looked vivid there looked washed out here. The intensity curve is
recalibrated: the panes thin out much further as the slider rises, so 50%
now looks like 50% ought to, and 100% is nearly bare gradient. Menus and
popovers were strengthened to match, so they stay in the same family
instead of reading as grey cards on a vivid page.

**Gradient Direction speaks standard CSS now**

The direction slider was 180° out from the reference's convention: their
180° and our 0° produced the same picture. The slider now reads the
standard way — 180° runs your first colour top to bottom — so the same
numbers mean the same direction in both apps. An existing theme keeps
working; if yours suddenly points the other way, nudge the slider by 180°.

**The app steps aside for the picker**

The customizer panel used to slide over the right edge of the window,
swallowing the search field and the members toggle while it was open. The
app now shifts left to make room, the way the reference does — everything
in the chat header stays visible and usable while you theme.

**In short**

- Custom colours roughly three times stronger at the same intensity
- Direction slider aligned to the standard CSS angle convention
- The window shifts aside for the picker instead of being covered
- The remove-colour dash is drawn heavier, so it reads as a control
- Panel slimmed to the reference's width
