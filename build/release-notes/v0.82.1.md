Much more scrolling before you are told you are viewing older messages

**The "You're Viewing Older Messages" notice waits far longer**

It still appeared sooner than it was worth seeing. The distance you have to
scroll back before it shows is now more than three times what it was: four
full screenfuls of conversation, rather than one and a bit.

Reading back through the last few exchanges — even a few pages of them — is
just reading, and the notice now stays out of it. It appears when you are far
enough back to have genuinely lost sight of the present, which is the only
time the "Jump To Present" button is worth having on screen.

As before, it measures screenfuls rather than a fixed distance, so it means
the same thing on a maximised window as on a small one.

**In short**

- You can now scroll back four screenfuls before the notice appears
- Short glances back — a few lines, half a page, even a few pages — no longer
  trigger it at all
