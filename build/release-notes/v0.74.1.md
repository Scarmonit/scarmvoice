Three things that named an action and then refused it

**Opening a conversation from the finder**

*Find or start a conversation* lists your conversations under **Jump to**, beside
your channels. Picking a channel there took you to it. Picking a conversation did
not — and it failed quietly, which is why it lasted:

- The panel opened titled "Conversation", with no messages and no way to tell
  which conversation it was supposed to be.
- Nothing ever loaded into it, because it had not actually asked for anything.
- No row in the sidebar lit up, so there was nothing on screen saying where you
  were. The only way in was to press the conversation in the list instead.

Picking one now opens it — the same conversation, the same name, the same
messages, its row highlighted, exactly as clicking it in the sidebar always did.

**Search results from another conversation**

With the search scope set to *All conversations*, results can come from any
conversation you are in, and every row says which one it came from. Clicking one
from a different conversation did not take you there:

- You stayed in the conversation you were already reading.
- After a moment you were told "that message is further back than we can jump"
  about a message that was not far back at all — it was simply somewhere else.

A result now opens the conversation it came from before it goes looking for the
message, which is what search has always done for channels.

**A failed download, and the button that would not retry**

You can press the Update Available pill while it is still downloading, and the
app remembers and installs the moment the download finishes. If that download
then failed:

- The pill correctly changed to say the download had failed, and offered
  *Try again*.
- The button under those words stayed greyed out and did nothing. Pressing the
  bar around it still worked, which is not something anyone would think to try.

Try again is now pressable when it says so. Your original press is remembered
either way, so a retry that succeeds installs without you pressing anything a
second time.
