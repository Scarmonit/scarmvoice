Custom theme colours without the grey wash

**The wash is gone**

The custom theme's colours were still coming through greyer than they
should, and the cause was found in how the layers combine. The app's panes
sit as translucent glass over your gradient, and translucent glass ADDS a
little of its own colour to everything beneath it — the panes were stock
grey, so every colour underneath was lifted toward grey. The parts of your
gradient that should read deep and saturated came out milky.

The panes now darken toward black as Color Intensity rises, so what they
add to the picture shrinks away and your colours keep their depth — deep
purples stay deep instead of going grey, exactly as measured against the
reference. The surfaces still step over each other the way they always
have; they just stop paying a grey tax on your colours.

**The remove button reads as a control now**

The "−" beside the eyedropper was rendering dimmer than its neighbour —
a single thin line splits across screen pixels and loses half its ink.
The dash is drawn heavier, and both buttons in the hex row now share the
same brighter colour, matched to the reference's weight.

**In short**

- Custom colours keep their saturation — the additive grey wash is fixed
- Deep gradient regions reach the depth the reference reaches
- The remove-colour dash and the eyedropper now match, both brighter
