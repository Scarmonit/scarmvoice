Settings navigation that keeps up with you, and sliders that behave

**The subsection list now follows your scrolling — on every tab**

- Scroll down through a settings page and the subsection list on the left highlights whatever you are actually looking at. Reach Visual Density and "Visual Density" lights up; keep going and the highlight moves to Color & Contrast on its own.
- Clicking a subsection scrolls smoothly to it, as before.
- This works on **every** tab with subsections now — Accessibility, Notifications, System, Account and Voice & Audio — not just Accessibility. Voice & Audio has gained its own list in the process.
- Reaching the very bottom always highlights the last subsection, so a short final section is no longer impossible to land on.

**Fixed: clicking a subsection made the page bounce**

- It jumped to the top and then scrolled down to whatever you clicked. Two things were fighting: switching pages resets the scroll to the top, and that reset was running even when you had not switched pages — a subsection click is on the page you are already reading. It only resets on a real page change now, so a click goes straight where you sent it in one motion.

**Every slider has tick marks**

- The tracks were plain bars with numbers floating above them, so each label pointed at a position nothing on the track agreed with. There is a mark on the track at every labelled value now.
- All of them, not just the Accessibility page: Microphone Volume, Speaker Volume and Speaking threshold in Voice & Audio had no scale at all, and were drawn permanently half-filled regardless of their actual value. They match the rest now.
- The first and last labels sit flush with the ends of the track instead of hanging off them.

**"Text size in chat" snaps to real sizes**

- The sizes it offers — 12, 14, 15, 16, 18, 20, 24 — are not evenly spaced, so dragging from 16 towards 18 used to rest on 17: a size no tick names. It now lands exactly on each one.
- Zoom level does the same, on the levels it actually offers (50, 67, 75, 80, 90, 100, 110, 125, 150, 175, 200).

**Zoom only applies when you let go**

- Dragging the Zoom slider used to rescale the whole window continuously while you were still moving it, which is disorienting when the thing you are dragging moves too. You can now drag from 100 to 110 freely and the zoom applies once, when you release the mouse. The scale under the slider still follows your thumb, so it stays live to use.

**Fixed: Saturation at 0% was applying 100%**

- Dragging it all the way down read "0%" and applied full colour — the one value where the number and the effect were opposites. It was a stray fallback treating zero as "not set". 0% now genuinely means 0%.
- One consequence worth knowing: at exactly 0% images and video go grey along with everything else. Above 0% they keep their colour as before. Full greyscale cannot be undone for media once it is applied, so "0%" is taken literally rather than quietly rounded up.
