The Filters form, measured against the real thing

Another measured pass, this time over the search **Filters** sheet — and one change that shows up everywhere.

**The fields are fields now**

- They were drawn as underlines: a line under each row and nothing else, no box, no fill. A form whose inputs are not drawn as inputs reads as a settings list, which is exactly what this looked like.
- Each one is a proper bounded box now — its own slightly darker fill, a hairline all the way round, 40px tall.
- The rule after every section is gone with them. Those rules were only there because the fields had no edges of their own; now that they do, the dividers were saying the same thing twice.

**Things that were not buttons, but should have been**

- **Add date** was plain text sitting on the sheet — there was nothing there to press. It is a real filled button now, the same height as the fields around it.
- **Cancel** was two points *darker* than the sheet it sat on and had no border, which made it very nearly invisible. It is raised out of the sheet with its own outline, like a button should be.

**Apply Filters says whether it will do anything**

- It used to be fully lit even when nothing had been chosen and pressing it would change nothing at all.
- It is now dimmed until pressing it would actually change something — and that includes *clearing* filters you had set, because removing them is a change too.

**The sheet itself**

- Capped at a sensible height with the body scrolling, instead of growing tall enough to run off a smaller window.
- A slightly taller header and a noticeably taller footer, so the two decisions at the bottom are not crammed into the thinnest band on the sheet.
- The "Filters" title was about 20% too small. Only the bold labels were — the explanatory lines under them were already right.
- The scrollbar inside a dialog stays visible and is light against the sheet. That is the opposite call from the one in the conversation, on purpose: there a permanent bar down the edge is clutter, here it is the only thing telling you there is more below the fold.

**The dimming behind every dialog**

- The background was not being dimmed enough, and it was dimming *unevenly* — the server rail went darker than the message column. That was a faint blue tint in the shade doing it: a tinted overlay affects dark surfaces differently from lighter ones.
- It is a plain black shade now, slightly stronger, and it dims every part of the app by exactly the same amount.
