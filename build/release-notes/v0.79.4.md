Orientation reshapes the panel, not just its contents

**What was still wrong**

The last release got the contents restacking, but the panel itself kept the
shape it already had. A me bar switched to Vertical stacked its profile and its
buttons into a column — down the left-hand end of a bar still as wide as the
whole sidebar, with the rest of it empty. That is not a vertical bar. It is a
horizontal bar with its contents pushed into one corner.

**Now the panel itself turns**

Switching a section's orientation reshapes the whole section to match:

- **The me bar, switched to Vertical**, becomes a tall narrow bar — profile at
  the top, then the microphone, then the headset, then the settings cog down the
  bottom.
- **The servers rail, switched to Horizontal**, becomes a wide short bar —
  Direct Messages on the left, then your server to the right of it.
- **Every section that offers Orientation does the same**, the channel header
  and the message box included.
- **Switch it back and you get back exactly the panel you had** — the same size,
  down to the pixel, not a fresh guess at it.

**Turning a section is a layout change now**

The default arrangement is a fixed grid, and a grid cannot be talked into a tall
narrow me bar — the space it lives in is one wide row across the bottom of the
window. So a section can only take a shape of its own once it has one, which
means switching orientation now makes your layout a custom one, exactly as
dragging or resizing a section always has. Everything else stays precisely where
it was at the moment you did it.

As before, the change applies the moment you click it and is saved with the
layout, including in saved layout templates.

**Also**

- A turned bar can now be dragged as narrow as it used to be short. The smallest
  size a section may be made turns with it, so a vertical bar is no longer held
  to the width a horizontal one needed.
