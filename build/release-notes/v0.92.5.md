A bug-hunt release: updates that come back, calls you actually left

**Updating**

- **The app no longer disappears on update day.** If you run ScarmVoice minimised to the tray, an update found at startup could quit the app instead of installing-and-relaunching it — the update applied silently, and ScarmVoice simply never came back until you launched it by hand. It now holds the process open for the installer handover, exactly as it was always meant to.
- If that handover fails anyway, the app takes the launch back properly. It used to come up with a window that could never be shown — a tray icon and nothing else.
- **Restart now comes back on screen.** Settings › Restart handed the launch flags of the session it was replacing to its replacement, so restarting an app that had started at sign-in (or after an update) dropped it back into the tray in front of you. A restart you clicked is a restart you are watching.
- The update screen's **Quit** button quits again. It had stopped doing anything: the app window now exists behind that screen, so closing it left ScarmVoice running invisibly.
- The update screen also comes down reliably. It was waiting on a window event that does not always arrive, so on some launches the app appeared underneath it for fifteen seconds.

**Voice**

- **Leaving a call during a reconnect actually leaves it.** While voice was reconnecting, Disconnect — and sign-out, a ban, and being taken over by another device — did nothing at all, and the automatic retry put you back in the call seconds later. In the sign-out case that meant a live microphone behind the login screen.
- **A reconnect no longer un-mutes you.** If the call dropped while you were muted or deafened, the rebuild opened your microphone and undeafened you for the length of the reconnect before putting your choice back. It now goes back in muted, as you left it.
- Two quick clicks on the camera button can no longer swallow the error the second one produces.

**Messages, DMs and the composer**

- Signing out with a request in flight no longer wipes your unread marks. The reply could land after the sign-out and overwrite the whole account's read positions with a single channel's.
- Reading back to the very start of a channel no longer sprouts a dead "Load earlier messages" row over the channel intro on the next refresh.
- Jumping to a message from search or the pinned panel no longer silently does nothing when a conversation happens to hold a message with the same id.
- Closing a conversation clears the header with it. The person you had been talking to used to stay above the empty state, with two buttons that acted on nothing.
- Leaving a conversation you had searched no longer scrolls the channel behind it to an unrelated position.
- With a custom Edit Mode layout, opening a DM no longer draws the message box at the wrong width and scale.

**Sound, notifications and settings**

- Join/leave announcements follow your output device. Prepared announcements kept playing out of the speaker that was selected when they were first fetched — the call in your headphones, arrivals out of the desktop speakers.
- Turning the taskbar **badge** off no longer turns taskbar **flashing** off with it. They are two switches and they now behave like two switches.
- The mic test releases the microphone when you close Settings while it is still opening. It could be left running, with the in-use light on and no way back to the Stop button.
- Arming a hotkey and then switching Settings panes no longer leaves the recorder listening — the next key you pressed anywhere was swallowed and saved as your push-to-talk binding.
- A dialog opening over another dialog restores keyboard focus correctly.
- The composer's attach button gets the solid glyph it was supposed to get last release.

**Under the hood**

- A realtime socket torn down mid-handshake could crash the main process. It cannot now.
- A read that follows one of your own writes is no longer served stale when the first attempt comes back as an error.
- A failed large upload closes the file it was reading instead of leaving the handle — and the file lock — behind.
