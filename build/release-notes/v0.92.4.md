About, polished — and it now tells you what is new to you

**Settings › About**

- The version leads the pane on a card of its own now, with the app name above it and a status badge beside it: a quiet green "Up to date", or a highlighted "Update available" when there is one. Whether you are current is a glance rather than a line of prose three rows down.
- The line under the badge appears only when it has something the badge cannot say — which version is waiting, how far a download has got, or that a check could not reach the feed.
- Release notes are cards with room to breathe: cleaner version and date type, a refined "Installed" badge, and a list that no longer reads as one long run of hairlines.

**What's new since your last version**

- The releases you have not read yet are marked. Everything published since the version you were on before your last update carries an accent edge and a "New" badge, and the heading says how many there are and which version it counted from — so after an update you can tell at a glance which notes are new to you and which you have already been through.
- The version you are running is marked "Installed" as before, and is always part of that group.

Everything that was in the pane is still in it: the version, the diagnostic log and "Open logs folder", "Check for updates", the collapsible list of the ten most recent releases, and the link to every release on GitHub.
