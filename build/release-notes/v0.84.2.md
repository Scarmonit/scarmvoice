Theme swatches you can see, and sharper edges on Onyx

**The theme picker is a row of swatches now**

Settings → Appearance no longer describes the themes in six rows of prose —
it shows them. A row of tiles under "Default Themes", each filled with the
actual colour of the theme it stands for, ordered light to dark: Light, Ash,
Dark, Onyx, then Sync with computer and Custom. The selected tile is marked
with a ring and a checkmark badge.

The descriptions didn't go away — hover any tile and its one-line
description appears under the row, and when nothing is hovered the line
describes the selected theme.

- Six tiles instead of six paragraphs — a quarter of the height
- Each tile shows its theme's real colour before you commit to it
- Hover for the description; the row reads at a glance either way

**Onyx: controls no longer fade into the black**

Onyx darkened everything by the same amount — including the outlines whose
job is to stand out against the surfaces they sit on. An unselected radio
ring had all but disappeared. Edges, rings and rules now keep most of their
brightness under Onyx, so every control still reads as something you can
press.

**And the Customize button looks like a button**

"Customize your theme…" was nearly the same shade as the panel behind it.
It has a proper raised fill and border now, the weight a secondary action
deserves.
