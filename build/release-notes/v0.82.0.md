Restarting an update brings the app back, and two fixes to getting in the way

**"Restart Now" brings the app back on screen**

If you had "Start minimized to the tray" turned on, clicking the blue Restart
Now button on an update installed it and then brought the app back into the
tray — so from where you were sitting, the button closed the app.

That setting is about a launch nobody asked for, at sign-in, in the
background. Clicking Restart Now is the opposite: you are at the window,
watching. The app now comes back the way it went away — visible if it was
visible, and still quietly into the tray if that is where it was.

**Dragging a picture in the chat no longer offers to upload it**

Picking up an image that had already been posted — to look at it, or to drag
it out to your desktop — put "Drop to upload" across the whole window, as if
you were adding a new file. An image already in the chat and an image dragged
in from a browser look identical to the app, so it could only tell them apart
by noticing where the drag began, which it now does.

Dragging pictures out of the app to your desktop or into another window still
works exactly as before, and so does dragging files and images in.

**You can scroll further back before being told you are in the past**

"You're Viewing Older Messages" appeared after barely any scrolling — less
than one screen, so glancing back at the message just above the one you were
reading was enough to be told you had left the present. It now waits until you
have put more than a screenful of conversation behind you, and it measures
that against the size of your window rather than a fixed distance.

**In short**

- Restart Now brings the app back visible if that is how you left it
- Dragging an already-posted picture no longer offers to upload it
- The "viewing older messages" notice waits for a real amount of scrolling
