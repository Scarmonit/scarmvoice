Four themes and a customizer: Ash, Onyx, and colours of your own

**Two new themes: Ash and Onyx**

Settings → Appearance now offers four looks instead of two.

- Ash is a softer, ashy grey — the dark theme with the lights turned up,
  easier in a bright room
- Onyx is near-pure black — made for OLED screens and dim rooms, where the
  regular dark theme still glows

Both keep every surface in the same layered arrangement, so nothing moves —
only the shades change.

**Customize your theme**

A new Custom option lets you colour the whole app yourself. Press "Customize
your theme…" in Appearance and the app behind the panel becomes a live
preview as you work:

- An Appearance toggle chooses whether your colours sit over a dark or a
  light base
- A full colour picker: a saturation and brightness square with a hue
  slider, a hex field for typing an exact colour, and an eyedropper for
  lifting one straight off your screen
- Add Color blends up to four colours across the window, shading from the
  first in the deepest surfaces to the last in the most raised
- A Color Intensity slider sets how strongly your colours are applied, from
  a hint of tint to fully saturated
- Surprise Me! rolls a random theme; Reset takes the customizer back to its
  starting point

Your text stays readable at every intensity — the customizer recolours the
surfaces and leaves the type alone — and your theme is saved with the rest
of your settings, so it survives restarts and updates. The window's own
title bar follows along, whichever theme you land on.
