Deep colours, readable text — the theme field finds its register

**The colours are dark and rich now, not bright**

The last release let the gradient through the panels at the picker's raw
brightness, which turned the app into mid-tone vivid colour — and took
text contrast down with it, to the point that timestamps disappeared into
the background.

The gradient now renders as a FIELD: your colours at full saturation but
deep — the way the reference draws a themed surface. Each colour is scaled
to a luminance ceiling rather than by a flat factor, so bright greens
darken hard while deep blues barely change, and every surface lands in the
same dark register whatever hue you pick. Text sits on dark ground again
and passes contrast where it was failing badly.

**Panels sit truly flush with the field**

The me-bar was reading a third hotter than the chat beside it — its
"raised" highlight was brightening the gradient. Under a custom theme,
elevation now comes entirely from borders and shadows, the reference's own
approach, so the me-bar, composer and panels are pixel-level continuous
with the field around them.

**In short**

- Themed surfaces are deep and saturated, not bright — matched to the
  reference's measured register
- Text contrast restored; timestamps are legible again
- A luminance ceiling per colour, so greens and blues both land right
- The me-bar and composer sit flush with the gradient, separation carried
  by their edges
