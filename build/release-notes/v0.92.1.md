Joining voice is fast now

**Voice**

- Joining a voice channel is near-instant. As your mouse heads toward the voice channel, the app quietly gets the entire connection ready — so by the time you click, the line to the server is already standing and the join itself takes a fraction of a second. Measured on a real call: about 0.2 seconds from click to connected when the app saw you coming, down from 2 to 5+ seconds before.
- You land in the room and hear everyone immediately; your microphone switches on right behind you instead of holding up the join. Getting ready never touches your microphone — it only opens once you are actually in the call.
- If a join is ever slow again, the app now keeps a step-by-step timeline of exactly where each join's time went, so it can be read from the log instead of guessed at.
