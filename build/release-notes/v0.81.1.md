Longer to read the ban notice before the app closes

**The ban notice now stays up for ten seconds**

When someone is banned, the full-screen notice naming who banned them used to
sit on screen for five seconds before the app closed itself. That is not long
enough to read it, register what it says and notice who it names — so it is
now ten full seconds, counted down on the notice itself.

Nothing else about a ban has changed: the call still ends the instant the ban
lands, the notice still appears immediately with its sound, and nothing behind
it can be clicked or typed into while it is up.

- The ban notice stays on screen for ten seconds instead of five
- The message board on the website waits the same ten seconds before returning
  to the sign-in page
