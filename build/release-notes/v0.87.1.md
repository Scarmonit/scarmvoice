The theme picker's sliders, strip and remove button, done properly

**The sliders stop glowing**

The customizer's two sliders were unstyled system controls — a near-white
track that was the brightest thing on the panel. They now wear the app's
own slider: a slim dark groove with a white thumb. Color Intensity fills
its travelled half, because an intensity is an amount; Gradient Direction
deliberately does not, because an angle is a position on a wheel — 181° is
not "more" than 90°.

**The colour strip works like a gradient editor**

The strip above the picker used to render the gradient at the chosen
angle, which squeezed the whole blend into its height and left the colour
chips bunched in a corner. The blend now always runs along the strip, and
each chip sits at the exact place its colour falls in the fade — so the
strip answers "where does each colour land", and the Direction slider
alone answers "which way does it point".

**A minus button to remove a colour**

Add Color finally has its opposite: a "−" beside the eyedropper removes
the selected colour. It hides itself while there is only one.

**In short**

- Slim dark slider tracks with white thumbs, matching the rest of the app
- Intensity fills as it grows; Direction stays unfilled, as an angle should
- Colour chips sit at their true positions along a horizontal blend
- Remove a colour with the new minus button beside the eyedropper
- The panel's buttons squared up to 40px
