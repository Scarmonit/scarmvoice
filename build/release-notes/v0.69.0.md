The conversation palette works from the keyboard now

**You can drive it without touching the mouse**

The panel behind "Find or start a conversation" listed everything and highlighted nothing, so there was no way to tell what Enter would do — and no reason to expect Enter to do anything.

- The first result is **selected** as soon as the panel opens, and stays selected as you type.
- **↑ ↓** move the selection, **Home** and **End** jump to the ends, and **Esc** closes.
- **Enter** acts on the selected row: a channel or an existing conversation opens it; a person starts a conversation with them straight away.
- Picking several people still makes a group — click them, as before.

**The footer stopped contradicting the panel**

It still said "Pick someone to message" while the first thing on screen was a list of channels. It now teaches the keys instead — and the **Cancel / Create DM** pair only appears once you have actually picked somebody, because until then there is nothing to confirm and a pair of buttons under a palette implies you have to click through it. When they do appear, they are drawn raised out of the card rather than two shades darker than it, which had made them nearly invisible.

**It reads like a palette**

- A **title above the panel**, naming what you are searching.
- The **search box is bigger again** — taller, with larger placeholder text. It is the whole point of the panel.
- **Result rows are quiet** until the selection is on them. Every row at near-white left the highlight with nothing to be brighter than.
- **Section headings are brighter**, and the panel is wider and shorter, which gives the "Channel" / "Conversation" column on the right room to sit clear of the names.
- Conversations show **the person's own face** instead of a generic @ — and so do the people you can start one with.
