A hung-up microphone, and a launch that shows itself

**Voice**

- **Hanging up during the first second of a call now actually hangs up.** Opening your microphone takes a moment at the start of a call, and Disconnect becomes clickable before that moment is over. Clicking it in there — or leaving from the tray, or signing out, or being taken over by another device — left the call half-torn-down: ScarmVoice said you had left, everyone else's member list said you had left, and your microphone stayed open and kept sending your voice into the room for the rest of the session, with the Windows in-use light on the whole time. Leaving now waits for the microphone to finish opening and then closes it properly.
- **Push-to-talk lets go when you switch windows, on the keyboards where it has to.** If your chosen key is one Windows will not share with other applications — the Menu key, Pause, and a few others — push-to-talk only works while ScarmVoice is in front, exactly as Settings says. Alt-tabbing mid-sentence used to leave the microphone open anyway, because the release was checking the wrong thing. Everyone else keeps holding through other windows, as before.
- The mic test releases the microphone when you leave the Voice & Audio page while it is still starting up. It could be left running with its Stop button on a page you were no longer looking at.

**Starting up**

- **The shortcut does something again on update day.** If you run ScarmVoice minimised to the tray, double-clicking it while an update was being found or downloaded showed nothing at all — no window, no tray icon, not even the update screen — for as long as that took. Clicking again did nothing either. A launch you performed now always has something on screen to look at. Starting at sign-in is still silent, as it should be.
- A settings save that Windows interrupted no longer costs you the settings. If antivirus or the search indexer held the file at the wrong moment, the next launch mistook the profile for a brand-new one, restored a years-old copy over it and signed you out of your account. It now recognises the half-finished save for what it is and recovers from the backup, which is what the backup was always for.

**Edit Mode**

- Picking a different layout from the dropdown no longer throws away an arrangement you have not saved. It asks first, the way leaving and deleting already did — and it no longer quietly disarms the question you get on the way out.

**Everywhere**

- Signing out while the app is still loading no longer brings the session back to life behind the sign-in card. On a slow connection the last of the startup could land after you had signed out and restart the polling, put you back in everyone's member list as online, and — with auto-join on — open your microphone and rejoin the call.
