The Windows 95 buttons are gone, and "Find or start a conversation" can now find

**Two buttons were wearing a 3D bevel**

"View Full Profile" and "Find or start a conversation" were drawn with a raised outset border — pale on the top-left, pure black on the bottom-right. That is the browser's own button, showing through: every other button in the app turns it off, and those two set a fill and a rounded corner and never thought about the border. There is no way to notice that from the rule you are writing, so it is switched off once for every button in the app rather than remembered 203 times. Nothing else changed appearance — only those two were showing it.

**"Find or start a conversation" now does both**

The button says two things and only did one: there was no way to reach **#general** from it. The panel it opens now lists, above the people:

- **Jump to** — your channels and the conversations you already have. Click one and you go there.
- **Start a conversation with** — the people, as before: pick one for a DM, pick a few for a group.

(While adding people to an existing group it stays as it was — "jump to #general" is not an answer to "who else should be in this group".)

**And it looks like a place you type**

- The panel is wider, and the **search box is the panel** — 60px tall with large placeholder text, rather than a small field tucked under a heading. It opens on a click and the next thing you do is type, so the box is what should meet you.
- The box is no longer a near-black hole in a grey card: it is a shade off the panel with a hairline round it, like every other field.
- The result rows are **flat** — no plate, no border, just a highlight on the one under your pointer. Twenty bordered boxes is what made the list read as a form.
- Rows are shorter, grouped under headings, and each says what it is on the right ("Channel", "Conversation", "Group").

**Profile panel**

"View Full Profile" sits at the reference's inset from the panel edges, and its label is a point larger and at full brightness — it is the only control in that panel and it was reading like a caption.
