Bans that end the session, and a sound for every voice action

**Getting banned now actually ends the session**

A ban used to be invisible to the person it happened to: their app sat there
working off what it already had, and if they were in voice they stayed in
voice. The moment the owner bans someone, that person's app now reacts
instantly, on every device they have open.

- Disconnected from the voice call the instant the ban lands
- A full-screen notice appears immediately, naming who banned them
- The interface is locked while the notice is up — nothing behind it can be
  clicked or typed into
- The ban sound plays as the notice appears
- After five full seconds, the app closes itself completely

The website's message board does the same, ending with a return to the
sign-in page instead of closing.

**A sound for every voice action**

Muting, unmuting, deafening and undeafening each play their own sound now —
heard only by you, never transmitted. Starting or stopping a screen share
plays a sound for everyone in the call, so a share never appears or vanishes
silently. A share that was already running when you joined stays quiet — the
start sound marks the moment someone begins.

- Mute, unmute, deafen and undeafen each have their own sound, just for you
- A screen share starting or stopping is heard by everyone in the call
- All of these follow the voice sounds toggle in Settings, like the join and
  leave chimes
